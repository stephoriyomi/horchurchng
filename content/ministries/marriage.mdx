"People often rush to treat a broken bone, but ignore a breaking soul; just because you can't see the wound doesn't mean it isn't real."

As a church, we are concerned about the general well-being of our congregation. Hence, the drive to establish a professional Guidance and Counselling Department. We onboard well-versed psychologists and counsellors, who are experienced in confidential and faith-based counselling and mentoring sessions, to ensure that people are helped to overcome their struggles and hurt.

Healthy marriages and families are of utmost importance to us, which is why we also offer both pre-marital and post-marital counselling sessions.

We are always available to listen, provide necessary support and guidance.
